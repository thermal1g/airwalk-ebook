Who the programme is for

For creators

who already get asked
  • You have between 15,000 and 200,000 followers.
  • You keep getting the same questions, in comments and in DMs.
  • You explain something concrete: a method, numbers, recipes, steps.
  • You don't have your own guide on the same topic yet.

If you already offer coaching or consulting, the guide sits next to it: it answers the basic questions, so you keep your time for your clients.

The full path

Nine steps,

from idea to launch

Every step has a clear result, and your part is always short. Nothing goes public without your written OK.

01
Week 1

Discovery

a conversation, not a long form
What we doWe look at what you post and what people ask you. We come back with a guide idea backed by real questions from your followers.
What you doYou choose how to tell us what you know: a 30-minute call or a written questionnaire you fill in when you have time.
About 30 minutes
02
Week 1

The agreement

simple, written, no surprises
What we doWe send you the terms, in plain language, on a single page.
What you doYou read them, open the account where you receive your earnings and confirm by email.
About 20 minutes
01You pay nothing, not now and not later.
02We earn together, in equal shares, automatically with every sale.
03The guide's content stays yours.
04Nothing is published without your written OK.
05The price changes only if we both agree.
03
Week 1

The outline

chapters built from real questions
What we doWe build 6 to 10 chapters. Each one answers questions your followers actually ask.
What you doYou read the outline and tell us what to add, cut or change.
About 15 minutes
04
Week 1

The content

whatever is easier: spoken or written
What we doWe send you a few clear questions for each chapter.
What you doYou choose how to answer: WhatsApp voice notes, chapter by chapter, or straight into a PDF with the questions already in it. You also send us the notes, numbers and photos you already have.
About an hour and a half
Our question
What do you check first when you pick up your car?
Your answer
A 2-minute WhatsApp voice note or a few lines in the PDF.
The result
A guide page with the checklist, ready to print.
05
Week 2

The writing

in your voice, fact-checked
What we doWe write the guide from your answers, in your tone. Anything about health, money or safety is checked against reliable sources.
What you doYou read the text once and leave comments right in the document.
About 45 minutes
06
Week 2

The design

a product, not a document
What we doCover, pages, charts and printable checklists, built on your identity. The guide comes in two versions: one for phones and one for print.
What you doOne round of feedback, then the final approval, in writing.
About 30 minutes
07
Week 3

Page and payment

a page just for your guide
What we doAn animated sales page, made for phones, with FAQs written for your audience. Payment happens right on the page: card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
What you doYou check the page before launch. That is all.
About 10 minutes
08
Week 3

Delivery

automatic, any time of day
What we doAfter paying, the buyer lands on a thank-you page, gets an email and downloads the guide from their account. It all runs on its own, even at night.
Tested before launchWe test the whole checkout with a test code. You get one too, so you see exactly what your buyers get.

Who does what

Your part is small.

the rest is on us

You

  • The call or the questionnaire
  • Answers per chapter (voice or PDF)
  • Reading the text
  • Approving the design
  • Launch posts and one mention a month
3–4 hours in total

Us

  • The idea and the outline
  • Questions per chapter
  • Writing and fact-checking
  • The full design
  • The sales page
  • Payment and delivery
  • The launch kit
  • The launch-day set
  • Monthly reports
  • Support for buyers

Money, simply

  • We earn together, in equal shares: half for you, half for us, from every sale (after the platform fee and VAT).
  • Your share lands in your account automatically, and you can withdraw it any time.
  • Each of us pays our own taxes.
  • We handle production, at no cost to you.
