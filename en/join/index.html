The criteria

Four things

we check for every creator
0 / 4 criteria met
  1. 01

    You have between 15,000 and 200,000 followers

    on your main account: Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.

    The audience needs to be big enough for demand, and still listening to you directly.

  2. 02

    At least one of your last 12 videos passed 20,000 views

    any of them, on any platform.

    It shows people actually see you, not just follow you.

  3. 03

    You teach something

    recipes, methods, steps, tips people put to use.

    A guide needs a method. If you mostly make entertainment, it is not the right time yet.

  4. 04

    You don't already sell a guide, course or paid community on the same topic

    1:1 coaching is fine.

    Our guide shouldn't compete with what you already sell.
